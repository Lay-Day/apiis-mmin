Entry Requirements

Those who wish to enrol should meet the following requirements:

  • Be a born-again believer
  • Be a member of a local church
  • Be already serving God in some capacity
  • Be between the ages of 23 and 65 years old (exceptions may be made)
  • Have completed a bachelor’s degree program or equivalent (exceptions may be made)
  • Understand and speak English
  • Be willing to learn (including learning to use technological applications)
  • Own a camera-enabled internet device (mobile phone, tablet, computer, etc.)
  • Have internet access capable of supporting participation in Zoom sessions
  • Be willing to make the commitment specified under Student Commitment

Each student must have:

  • A WhatsApp account
  • A Gmail account
  • A Zoom account

Each student may register for one program (branch) only. If a student is found to be registered in more than one branch, all of that student’s registrations will be cancelled, all results will be void, and the student will not be admitted to APIIS again.

If you meet these requirements and want to master your ministry, we invite you to enrol in our MMin program!

Student Commitment

Each week, students are required to

  • Commit approximately 15 hours for self-study; and to
  • Attend and actively participate in a 2-hour class over Zoom, where they will interact with their classmates and can ask questions of the instructors.
